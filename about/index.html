About Who we are

Practical thinking. Connected solutions. A clearer way forward.

Quantiva Tech Digital is a Dubai-based technology and business solutions company working across software, web, AI, digital marketing, advertising, consultancy and technology supply. We believe good solutions begin with listening: understanding the people, processes and goals behind every requirement.

Our approach

We combine technical capability with a human understanding of business. Whether the requirement is a website, a software application, an AI-enabled workflow, a digital campaign or management guidance, we focus on the purpose behind the project. The result should not simply be a deliverable; it should be something useful, understandable and aligned with the organisation's next step.

Because many projects cross disciplines, we organise our work so that technology, design, marketing and advice are planned together. That keeps decisions consistent and reduces the hand-offs that slow projects down.

Our vision

To be a trusted partner for organisations seeking thoughtful technology, meaningful digital experiences and practical business solutions.

Our mission

To make technology and digital capabilities more useful to businesses by delivering considered solutions, clear advice and collaborative service.

Hands-on experience in digital and technology services.

Our leadership brings over 20 years of hands-on experience in digital and technology services. That experience shapes how we scope projects, advise on options and deliver work that is practical, maintainable and built around your goals.

Method How we work

A simple, transparent process.

Every engagement follows the same four steps, so you always know where a project stands and what comes next.

01

Listen

We start with your objectives, users, existing systems and constraints before suggesting anything.

02

Define

We agree scope, options and trade-offs in plain language, confirmed in a written proposal or quotation.

03

Deliver

We build, test and review in stages, so you can see progress and give feedback along the way.

04

Support

We hand over clearly and, where agreed, continue to support, manage and improve what we delivered.

Commitments What we aim to provide

What you can expect from us.

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every project, large or small.

  • A clear written scopeObjectives, deliverables, timeline and commercial terms agreed before work begins.
  • Plain-language communicationOptions, risks and trade-offs explained without unnecessary jargon.
  • Responsible handling of informationCare with your data, with confidentiality and privacy considered from the start.
  • Solutions built to lastWork that is maintainable, documented and able to adapt as your organisation changes.
  • Honest adviceIf a simpler or different approach suits you better, we will say so.
