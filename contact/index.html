FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Quantiva Tech Digital provide?

We work across software and IT solutions, web design, social media applications, education and training software, social media marketing, portal development, online selling, website advertising, advertising design and production, management consultancy, AI research consultancy, AI development and computer equipment trading.

Do you work with businesses across the UAE?

We are based in Dubai and can discuss requirements from organisations across the UAE. Project delivery arrangements and service availability are confirmed during the initial discussion.

Can you support a project that needs more than one service?

Yes. Where a requirement spans areas such as software, web, AI and digital marketing, we can discuss the work as a connected project and clarify the scope, responsibilities and delivery stages.

Do you develop custom software and AI solutions?

We can assess custom software and AI requirements based on the business problem, users, data, integrations and intended outcome. The proposed approach depends on feasibility and project scope.

Can you help improve an existing website or digital platform?

Yes. We can discuss the current platform, identify the issues or opportunities you have noticed and recommend an appropriate scope of work.

Do you provide digital marketing and advertising services?

We support social media marketing, website-based advertising and advertising creative development. Channel selection, campaign objectives, budgets and reporting are agreed according to the brief.

Can you supply computers and peripheral equipment?

We can discuss computer and peripheral equipment requirements. Product specifications, availability, warranty and delivery terms are confirmed in the quotation.

How do we get started?

Contact us with a short description of your requirement. We will arrange an initial discussion, clarify the objectives and determine whether a proposal or quotation is the appropriate next step.
