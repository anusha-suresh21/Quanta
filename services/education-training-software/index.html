Education & Training Computer Software

Technology that supports better learning experiences.

Learning programmes work best when the technology supports the people delivering and receiving them. We help education and training organisations explore software solutions for course information, learning resources, learner access and training administration. The right approach depends on the programme, audience and delivery model, so we begin by understanding how learning happens before shaping the digital solution.

Talk to Our Team About This Service

What this can include

  • Course informationPresenting programmes and schedules clearly.
  • Learning resourcesOrganising content for learner access.
  • Learner accessSimple, appropriate ways for learners to get to what they need.
  • Training administrationDigital support for the operations behind delivery.

How a project runs

  1. Learning needsUnderstand your programmes, audiences and delivery model.
  2. Platform designPlan course structure, learner access and administration tools.
  3. Build & set upConfigure or develop the platform and organise your content.
  4. Launch & supportGo live and support trainers and learners through the first cycle.

Exact deliverables, timeline and commercial terms are confirmed in a project proposal or quotation.

Who this service may be for

Businesses, start-ups, established organisations, education and training providers, and teams looking for a considered solution to a specific operational or digital requirement.
