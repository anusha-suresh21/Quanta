Portal

Bring the right people, information and services together.

A well-planned portal gives users a more organised way to access information and complete tasks. We work with organisations to define portal users, permissions, content structures and required functionality. Whether the portal is intended for customers, employees, partners or a specific community, we focus on making the experience clear, secure by design and aligned with the processes it needs to support.

Talk to Our Team About This Service

What this can include

  • User and permission planningDefining who can see and do what.
  • Content structureOrganising information so it is easy to find.
  • Workflow functionalitySupporting the tasks the portal exists for.
  • Secure-by-design approachConsidering access and data handling from the start.

How a project runs

  1. Users & permissionsDefine who the portal is for and what each role can see and do.
  2. Structure & workflowsOrganise content and map the tasks the portal must support.
  3. Build & secureDevelop the portal with access control and data protection designed in.
  4. Launch & extendGo live, gather feedback and plan the next features.

Exact deliverables, timeline and commercial terms are confirmed in a project proposal or quotation.

Who this service may be for

Businesses, start-ups, established organisations, education and training providers, and teams looking for a considered solution to a specific operational or digital requirement.
