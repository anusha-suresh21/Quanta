Social Media Applications Development & Management

Digital communities need more than a place to connect.

We support the development and management of social media applications and related digital experiences. This can include planning user journeys, defining platform features, supporting application design and coordinating ongoing management activities. We consider how people discover content, interact with one another and use platform features, with the aim of creating a clear and dependable experience.

Talk to Our Team About This Service

What this can include

  • User journey planningMapping how people discover content and interact.
  • Feature definitionDeciding which platform features serve your community.
  • Application design & developmentBuilding clear, dependable social experiences.
  • Ongoing managementCoordinating day-to-day management activities after launch.

How a project runs

  1. Concept & journeysDefine the community, its purpose and how people will use the platform.
  2. Feature designDecide which features serve users and design the experience.
  3. Build & testDevelop the application and test usability, performance and privacy.
  4. Launch & manageRelease the platform and support day-to-day management where agreed.

Exact deliverables, timeline and commercial terms are confirmed in a project proposal or quotation.

Who this service may be for

Businesses, start-ups, established organisations, education and training providers, and teams looking for a considered solution to a specific operational or digital requirement.
